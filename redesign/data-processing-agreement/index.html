Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: July 22, 2026

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the Terms of Service between you ("Client," "Controller") and MVS Solves ("Processor") whenever a Service — such as an online booking system or AI assistant — involves MVS Solves processing personal information belonging to your own end-customers, on your behalf and on your documented instructions.

This DPA applies automatically wherever it is relevant to your plan; a separately signed copy is available on request for clients who need one for their own compliance records — contact martin@mvssolves.com.

1. Roles

You are the Controller of your end-customers' personal information (for example, names, contact details, and booking history collected through a booking system or AI assistant built for your business). MVS Solves is the Processor, acting strictly on your documented instructions as set out in your project scope, this DPA, and the Terms of Service.

2. Scope and Nature of Processing

MVS Solves processes such personal information solely to provide the Services you've purchased — for example, to operate a booking system, run an automation, or power an AI assistant configured for your business. We do not use your end-customers' personal information for our own purposes, or sell it.

3. Confidentiality

MVS Solves ensures that anyone processing personal information under this DPA (including any subprocessor under Section 5) is subject to a duty of confidentiality.

4. Security

MVS Solves implements reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including access controls, encrypted connections where applicable, and use of reputable third-party infrastructure providers.

5. Subprocessors

You authorize MVS Solves to engage third-party subprocessors (for example, hosting, scheduling, payment, or automation platforms) to deliver the Services. MVS Solves remains responsible for subprocessors' compliance with the obligations in this DPA. A current list of subprocessors is available on request.

6. Assistance

MVS Solves will provide reasonable assistance to help you respond to data subject requests (access, deletion, correction) and to meet your own obligations under applicable data protection law, to the extent this relates to personal information processed under this DPA.

7. International Transfers

Where personal information is transferred outside the country in which it was collected (for example, to US-based hosting or processing infrastructure), MVS Solves relies on the subprocessor's own compliance mechanisms (such as standard contractual clauses, where applicable) for that transfer.

8. Deletion or Return

On termination of the applicable Services, MVS Solves will, at your request, delete or return personal information processed on your behalf, except where retention is required by law.

9. Audit

On reasonable written notice, MVS Solves will provide information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.

10. Precedence

In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Terms of Service regarding the processing of personal information, this DPA controls.
