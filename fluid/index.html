Engineering note

Fluid without a pressure solve

The constraint

A real fluid simulation enforces incompressibility with a pressure projection — typically 20–40 Jacobi iterations per frame, each its own render pass. That is a serious frame budget for what is, here, a decorative background.

Why the obvious route fails

Full stable-fluids gives physical accuracy nobody looking at a marketing site can perceive, and spends most of the GPU getting there. On a mid-range phone it simply does not hold 60fps.

What I did instead

The curl of a scalar field is divergence-free by construction — ∇·(∇×F) = 0 identically. Advect dye through curl-noise and you get motion that cannot converge or diverge, so it behaves like an incompressible fluid without ever solving for pressure. Two framebuffers ping-pong: sample where the fluid came from, fade, inject under the cursor.

The trade-off I accepted

This is a visual fluid, not a simulation. There is no obstacle interaction and no true boundary response — you cannot put a wall in it and expect the flow to react correctly. For a background, that is the right trade. For a physics tool, it would be the wrong one.
