02 — The room

Iron, chalk,
a concrete floor

Unit 7, Wallis Road

Two hundred square metres, eight platforms, and a coach who can tell you what you lifted last Tuesday without looking it up. If you want a sauna and a smoothie bar we are the wrong gym, and we will tell you that on the phone rather than after you have joined.

05 — First session

What actually
happens

Roughly 70 minutes

You arrive

Ade meets you at the door. No tour, no clipboard, no sales desk in the corner.

00:00on the hour Kit: trainers, whatever you own

Ten minutes talking

Injuries, what you have lifted before, what you actually want. Written down, kept on file.

00:05five in No fitness test

You lift

An empty bar first, whatever your background. Squat, hinge, press — the coach watches and adjusts.

00:15the session Empty bar is 20 kg

You leave with a plan

Three sessions written out, with the weights to start at. Yours whether you join or not.

01:00at the end No card taken

One follow-up, then nothing. If it is not for you that is a fine outcome, and better established on day one than in month three when you have stopped coming but kept paying.

06 — Free session

Come and lift once

No card, no tour, no pitch

Turn up, train with a coach, decide afterwards. If it is not for you we will say thanks and that will be the end of it.
