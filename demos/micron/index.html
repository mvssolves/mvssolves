Four stages,
and a reading after each

  1. Stage 01

    Decontaminate

    Chemical iron removal, then a clay bar. Anything still bonded to the paint becomes a scratch the moment a pad touches it.

    No material removed

  2. Stage 02

    Measure and map

    Depth gauge across forty points, photographed under the lamp, marked on a panel diagram you get a copy of.

    Baseline recorded

  3. Stage 03

    Correct

    Test spot first, always. The least aggressive combination that clears the defect is the one that gets used on the rest of the car.

    3–6 µm typical

  4. Stage 04

    Protect and re-measure

    Coating applied, panel re-read, both numbers on the invoice. If we took more than we said we would, that is on us and we will tell you.

    Final reading logged
