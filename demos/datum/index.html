Scope

Seven things. We subcontract none of them, which is why the schedule holds.

01

Clearing & grubbing

Vegetation, stumps, root balls and buried debris off the pad. Hauled, not buried on site, and manifested.

Typ. 1–3 days / acre

02

Mass excavation

Cut and fill balanced on site where the soils report allows it. Import and export priced per loaded yard, both directions shown.

Up to 4,000 CY / day

03

Over-ex & recompaction

Remove unsuitable, moisture condition, replace in eight-inch lifts. Compacted to 95% per ASTM D1557 and tested by your engineer, not ours.

95% rel. compaction

04

Wet utilities

Storm, sewer and water, four to twenty-two feet. Shored per OSHA at depth rather than benched at the last minute.

4–22 ft depth

05

Grading to subgrade

Rough through finish, GPS on both dozers. Handed over at ±0.10 ft with a survey to prove it.

±0.10 ft tolerance

06

Shoring & retention

Soldier pile and lagging, shotcrete faces, and the temporary shoring that lets the next trade start on time.

To 26 ft retained

07

Erosion control

SWPPP implementation, BMP maintenance and the inspection log that keeps the regional board off the job.

QSP on staff

How a job runs

  1. 1

    Walk it

    On site inside four business days, with your plans and the soils report already read.

  2. 2

    Take off

    Quantities calculated from the grading plan and shown to you as quantities, not as one number.

  3. 3

    Bid

    Unit prices, assumed haul distance, assumed import source. Every assumption named so you can argue with it.

  4. 4

    Mobilize

    Equipment and a superintendent on site the morning we said, not the week we said.

  5. 5

    Daily log

    Loads, hours, weather and delays, emailed at end of shift. Change orders never arrive as a surprise.
