03 / Our view

A case is an argument,
not a performance.

Most cases are lost on the documents, months before anyone stands up.

So the work happens early: reading all of it, finding the three documents that decide the thing, and saying plainly what they mean. That conversation belongs in week one, not in the corridor outside court on the morning of the hearing.

If the documents are against you, you get that in writing. Clients told early come back; clients told late, on the steps, do not.
