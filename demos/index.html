Every business we build for asks the same question first: what will it actually look like? These are the answer. Six visual directions, each taken far enough that you can walk through it instead of imagining it.

Being straight with you: each of these is a single page, not a finished website. One page, real code, real motion — built to settle the look, not to run a business.

The businesses are invented. The photography is licensed stock, and yours would be your own. What is real is the design system underneath each one: the palette, the type, the spacing, the way it moves. That is the part that carries over.

Point at a direction. We build your site in it, properly, with your pages, your words and your work — here is what that includes.

What a project is

Picking the look is the easy half. This is the rest of it.

Scope moves with the business — a restaurant and an excavation contractor do not need the same site. What follows is the base every build starts from, before we have talked about yours at all.

  1. Unlimited pages

    No page count and no per-page charge. If the business needs eleven service pages, a location page for each city and a careers page, it gets them.

  2. The words, written for you

    You are not handed a template with lorem ipsum in it. Headlines, body copy, calls to action, the page titles nobody enjoys writing — written, then rewritten until they sound like you.

  3. Hosting, domain and certificate

    Deployed, hosted and secured on our side. You never log into a registrar, renew a certificate, or find out the site is down from a customer.

  4. Changes, for as long as you are with us

    Sites go stale the day changing a price becomes a quote. Send the change and it gets made — new prices, new photos, a page for the thing you started offering last week.

  5. SEO built in, not sold back later

    Structured data, metadata, sitemap, clean URLs, Core Web Vitals and the local signals that decide whether you show up in the map pack. In from the first page.

  6. The automations behind it

    Booking, quote requests, lead routing, follow-up that actually goes out. The half of MVS that is not the website — whatever is currently being done by hand at nine at night.

  7. Analytics, forms and what you already run on

    Analytics that answer real questions, forms that arrive where you read them, and your existing CRM, calendar and inbox wired in. Not left as homework.

  8. Built to hand over cleanly

    Mobile first, keyboard accessible, fast on a phone on cell data, and readable code underneath. If we ever part ways you are not held hostage by it.

About the motion in these six. The WebGL, the scroll work, the calculators — all of that is on the table, and it is included where it earns its place. On some businesses it is the reason someone stays and books. On others it gets between a customer and a phone number, and we will tell you so rather than sell it to you.
