answers.

Do I need a new roof first?

If your roof has under seven years left, we'll say so at survey and hold the install. Putting a 25-year array on a failing roof costs you twice.

What happens on a cloudy week?

The system stays grid-tied. You draw from the grid or the battery as needed — production dips, supply doesn't.

Who handles the utility paperwork?

We do, end to end: AHJ permit, plan check corrections, interconnection application and PTO. You sign the design and the final acceptance.

Is the estimate above binding?

No. It's modelled from your bill and roof area. The binding number comes after the survey, and it has been within 6% of the estimate on our last 200 jobs.

Can I add a battery later?

Yes — we pre-wire every install for storage at no extra cost, so a later battery is a half-day job instead of a rewire.
